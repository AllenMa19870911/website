About Me

I am Hao-Shang Ma. I was born in Taipei, Taiwan, R.O.C..

Hao-Shang Ma received the B.S. and M.S. degree in Computer Science and Engineering from Yuan Ze University at 2010 and 2013 respectively. He studied in the institute of Computer and Communication Engineering for PhD in National Cheng Kung Unversity and received the PhD degree in July 2022. Currently, he is an assiatant professor in Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering at National Taichung University of Science and Technology. Since January 2022, he is the Executive Secretary of the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET) - Taipei Network. His research interests include Artificial Intelligence, Data Mining, Social Network Analysis, Recommender Systems, and Nature Language Processing.

Research Area

My research interests center on artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and recommender systems, with a particular emphasis on developing intelligent models that can understand complex data, learn user preferences, and support decision-making in real-world applications. My work combines machine learning, deep learning, data mining, and multimodal analysis to address problems involving perception, prediction, classification, recommendation, and human-centered intelligent services.

社群網路分析（Social Network Analysis）

Due to the large amount of data generated due to the social interaction of a large number of users, the research on social network analysis can also be combined with the technology of cloud computing (Cloud Computing) to deal with it. In addition to pursuing the accuracy of social network analysis degree and efficiency, how to propose an algorithm that can be applied to huge amounts of data, such as various dynamically updated community analysis algorithms (Dynamic Social Network), community influence reverse sampling algorithm (Reverse Influence Sampling), or The use of cloud computing technology to improve computing efficiency in actual large-scale social network applications has also been the focus of development in recent years. On the other hand, thanks to the rapid development of Deep Learning and Natural Language Processing in recent years, social network analysis has also combined these technologies to develop. Network Embedding uses deep learning and neural network models To simulate the interpersonal relationship of users in the community, use low-dimensional representation to present user characteristics, replace the original community connection matrix, and have better performance for user grouping and calculation of user similarity. Sentiment Analysis applies language models to analyze the direction of public opinion and help governments or enterprises manage crises. When a major public crisis occurs in society, relevant discussions often spread quickly on social media platforms in a short period of time, affecting the public's trust in the government or enterprises. Social Recommendation also uses deep learning technology to learn the user's social behavior on the social network, the correlation between users and user preferences, so as to help the recommendation system to provide recommendations that are closer to the user's preferences. content. In the future, it will be one of the most mainstream trends to apply more deep learning technologies to simulate more complex social relationships and user behaviors to improve the efficiency of social network analysis in various fields on these issues.

人工智慧應用（Artificial Intelligence Application）

In artificial intelligence, my research focuses on the design and application of advanced learning models, including deep neural networks, graph neural networks, transfer learning, reinforcement learning, and multimodal learning architectures. I am especially interested in integrating domain knowledge into model design so that artificial intelligence systems can better reflect the characteristics of specific application fields. My previous studies have applied artificial intelligence techniques to human action recognition, environmental sound analysis, intelligent control, image understanding, health management, esports behavior analysis, and financial trend recognition. Through these studies, I aim to improve not only model accuracy but also robustness, adaptability, interpretability, and practical usability.

自然語言處理（Natural Language Processing）

In natural language processing, my research explores semantic representation, text understanding, language generation, and the interaction between textual and visual information. This direction includes the use of Transformer-based architectures, large language models, and vision-language models for tasks such as image captioning, intelligent assistants, multimodal reasoning, and automated information analysis. I am particularly interested in how language models can be integrated with external knowledge, visual features, user context, and domain-specific information to generate more accurate, relevant, and explainable outputs.

推薦系統（Recommender Systems）

In recommender systems, my research focuses on user preference modeling, future preference prediction, context-aware recommendation, sequential recommendation, and personalized decision support. Rather than relying solely on historical user behavior, my work seeks to incorporate social relationships, emotional states, environmental conditions, and dynamic interaction data into recommendation models. These techniques have been applied to areas such as social recommendation, personalized music recommendation, health and dietary recommendation, esports item and strategy recommendation, and intelligent financial analysis. Some of my studies also incorporate deep reinforcement learning to enable recommendation systems to continuously adjust their strategies according to user feedback and changing environments.
